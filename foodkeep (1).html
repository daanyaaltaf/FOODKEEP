The system

Three habits, not a diet plan

Foodkeep doesn't ask you to meal-prep or feel guilty. It just keeps the receipt.

01

Stock your pantry

Log what you bring home and when it expires. Takes about as long as unpacking the bag.

02

Get nudged

Foodkeep flags what's about to turn, sorted by how much time you've got left — not by guesswork.

03

See the receipt

Every toss gets logged with a reason. Over a few weeks, the pattern tells you more than the pile ever did.
